# TripMate – Smart & Personalized Travel Planner for India

TripMate is an all-in-one travel planning platform designed to make travelling across India easier, smarter, and more affordable. It helps users plan personalized trips based on their budget, available time, interests, and travel preferences.

Planning a trip to an unfamiliar destination can be confusing and time-consuming due to scattered information about tourist attractions, local food, accommodation, transportation, shopping, and cultural experiences. TripMate aims to solve this problem by bringing essential travel information together in one place.

The platform helps travellers create customized itineraries, explore popular destinations, discover local experiences, estimate travel expenses, and manage their trip budget efficiently. It focuses on providing a convenient and user-friendly travel planning experience, especially for budget-conscious travellers and first-time visitors.

**Key Objectives:**
- Personalized travel recommendations.
- Budget-friendly trip planning.
- Customized day-wise itineraries.
- Destination and tourist attraction discovery.
- Local food and cultural experience suggestions.
- Estimated travel expense management.
- Simplified travel planning in one platform.

**Our Vision:** To make travel across India more accessible, organized, affordable, and enjoyable through smart and personalized planning.
